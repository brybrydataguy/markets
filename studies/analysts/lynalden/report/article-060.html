This rotation report mixes sound infrastructure observations with weakly specified stock timing. Mining relocation and technical Fed adjustments were verified. The explicit three-year healthcare comparison split sharply: CI slightly beat AMGN, while BMY severely lagged. AMLP versus VNQ was favorable in a disclosed sensitivity window, but that window was not specified by the author. Dollar bearishness faced sustained contrary outcomes. Technology development and corporate growth remain separate from equity returns; a network image is insufficient evidence of decentralization.
Coverage. Read every section and all image labels. Retained fiscal/rate mechanisms, conditional oil and bitcoin views, adoption/mining claims, and every stock or sector thesis supporting the rotations. Pure position listings and biographies excluded.
