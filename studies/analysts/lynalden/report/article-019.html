019-C09 · forecast · source blocks 77

Bitcoin miners adjust after halving

Mixed
HorizonMay 2020 halving
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted

Lower subsidy squeezes inefficient miners at unchanged price/difficulty. The protocol subsequently adjusts difficulty, so actual exit magnitude needs hash-rate and profitability data; it is not guaranteed by subsidy arithmetic alone.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: See reason; no return inferred.

Timing: Original horizon retained: May 2020 halving

Mechanism: Outcome evidence does not independently identify causation.

Reviewer confidence: medium. Thesis family: bitcoin-halving-miner-exit.

Independent evidence
  • Bitcoin halving and difficulty mechanics
    Subsidy halves every 210,000 blocks; 2020 cut 12.5 to 6.25 BTC. Difficulty retargets every 2016 blocks, so miner exit can lower difficulty. Reduced issuance is not a protocol requirement for permanently higher mining cost or market price.
