188-C11 · explanation · source blocks 75, 77, 121, 122

Dollar debts amplify currency stress.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Local debt value equals dollar principal multiplied by local-currency units per USD; a rise in that exchange rate increases the burden.

Assumptions: Debt fixed in USD; Revenue in local currency; No offsetting currency hedge or dollar asset

For an unhedged borrower with local-currency revenues, dollar appreciation increases the local-currency value of fixed dollar obligations. Dollar revenues, hedges and reserves can offset this effect; it is not uniform across all emerging markets.
