Inflation thresholds and two growth selections.

The spring inflation threshold and Bitcoin new-high call were successful. Broad student forgiveness failed to be implemented, and the explicit multi-year Adobe and Alibaba investment cases performed poorly. Their disclosed valuation and political risks deserve weight in the outcome.
