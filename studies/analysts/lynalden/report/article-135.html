Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

The payer deposit and bank reserves decline by the payment while the Treasury account rises equally. This proves the narrow transfer effect, not market-price transmission.

Assumptions: Tax payment is from a commercial-bank deposit to the Treasury Fed account; No offsetting government spending or Fed operation

Tax transfers into Treasury cash reduce private balances absent offsetting flows; maturity composition changes who supplies financing. The arithmetic does not by itself forecast asset prices.

135-C12 · explanation · source blocks 47

Weak yen redistributes purchasing power.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Yen export receipts equal e*R and yen import costs e*M; a higher e increases both. This proves incidence before pass-through and behavioral effects.

Assumptions: Foreign receipts R and import obligations M are fixed in dollars; No hedging or offsetting cost/volume adjustment; e is yen per dollar

A weaker currency increases local revenue from unchanged foreign receipts and raises local import costs, subject to hedging and pass-through. This is conditional arithmetic, not a stock-return forecast.
