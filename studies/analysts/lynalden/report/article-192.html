192-C03 · explanation · source blocks 16, 17

Dollar strength restrains global liquidity.

Mixed
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Dollar-translated stock equals local money stock multiplied by the conversion rate; translation alone reduces the measured dollar stock.

Assumptions: Foreign-currency money quantities unchanged; Exchange rate expressed as dollars per foreign unit falls

Dollar conversion mechanically lowers the dollar value of unchanged foreign-currency money stocks when the dollar rises. Dollar borrowers may also face tighter debt service. That does not make translated global M2 a direct measure of investable capital or identify Warsh remarks as the cause.
