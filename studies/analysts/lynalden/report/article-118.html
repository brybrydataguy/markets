Growth vs Value Equities Update

The Egypt devaluation horizon was a clear success, and the Grayscale procedural interpretation was careful. The BRICS entry headline overstated completed membership, although the Argentina caveat was explicit. Visa and HDFC have sharply divergent interim outcomes under the same unfinished five-year horizon. Uranium’s near-term follow-through was strong; oil and growth-versus-value narratives require separation of commodity prices, equity returns and causal explanations. Neutral exits are not bearish predictions.
