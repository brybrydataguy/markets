171-C12 · explanation · source blocks 87

Foreign sales reduce direct US tariff exposure.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Direct tariff cost equals affected imported value times rate; reducing the affected sales share reduces that direct cost.

Assumptions: Same import cost and tariff rate for US sales; Foreign sales not subject to that US tariff; Other operations unchanged

For otherwise comparable sourcing and margins, a smaller share of goods sold into the tariff-imposing country reduces direct tariff exposure. Supply-chain routing, retaliatory duties and currency effects still matter; geography alone does not determine total profit impact.
