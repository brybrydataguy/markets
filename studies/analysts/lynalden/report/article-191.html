191-C16 · explanation · source blocks 107, 111, 113

Expensive shares provide acquisition currency.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Shares issued equal purchase price divided by share price; a higher price reduces percentage dilution for the same target.

Assumptions: Purchase consideration fixed in dollars; Consideration paid in acquirer shares

For a fixed purchase price, a higher acquirer share price reduces the number of new shares issued. Space X’s disclosed stock-funded Cursor option illustrates the channel. This does not guarantee the acquisition creates value or that the elevated multiple persists.
