Global Equity Valuations Deep Dive Analysis

Global valuations meet fiscal and liquidity constraints.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Not testable · 17Mixed · 10Right · 8Evidence needed · 8Wrong · 1Too early · 7
The two-year Bitcoin forecast and ETF sequencing worked. The real-equity recovery delay faces strong contrary evidence, while the broad Wilshire comparison requires index-specific verification. International holdings appreciated but lagged the US in the evaluator window, and Itau has not completed its five-year horizon. The framework is strongest when separating liquidity components and valuation composition; it is less decisive when fiscal dominance or capital-flow narratives become broad timing explanations.

Dollar debt relief

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Local payment = e*D, so a fall in e reduces the payment at fixed D.

Assumptions: Fixed dollar payment D; Exchange rate e in local currency per dollar; No hedge or offsetting dollar revenue

For a fixed USD liability, fewer local-currency units per dollar lower local debt service, assuming no offsetting hedge or revenue change. Commodity revenue exposure can change the actual net effect.
