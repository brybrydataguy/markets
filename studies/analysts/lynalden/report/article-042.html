042-C15 · explanation · source blocks 94

Liquidity proves bank capitalization

Wrong
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted

Cash and Treasuries are assets; capitalization is the equity/loss-absorbing financing supporting assets. The Fed separately measures CET1. Banks could be well capitalized, but a high liquid-asset share is not proof of that proposition.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: See reason; no return inferred.

Timing: No fixed endpoint supplied.

Mechanism: Outcome evidence does not independently identify causation.

Reviewer confidence: medium. Thesis family: bank-liquidity-vs-capital.

Independent evidence
  • December 2020 bank stress tests · 2020-12-18
    Aggregate CET1 was 12.2% in Q2, falling to modeled minima 9.6% and 9.7% under two adverse scenarios. Increased loss reserves reduced subsequent required provisions. This measures capital separately from holdings of liquid assets.
  • How money is created · 2019-10-01
    Reserves are electronic central-bank settlement money. Capital is a separate resource that absorbs loan-default losses; both liquidity and capital constraints apply to banks.
