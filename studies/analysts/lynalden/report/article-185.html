Claim-by-claim assessment

185-C01 · empirical · source blocks 8

One-fifth of global gas disappeared.

Wrong
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted

The gas denominator is incorrect: IEA’s roughly 20% figure concerns LNG, which is a subset of total natural gas. The adjacent oil statement is less clear-cut: blocked chokepoint export flows are not the same as net world production lost. IEA’s March 12 projection of an 8mb/d net oil supply decline predates the article and is a forecast, not a final observed March result. The gas error should not be used to dismiss the severity of the oil-flow disruption.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: See assessment; evidence is limited to the stated proposition.

Timing: No fixed deadline stated.

Mechanism: Price direction alone does not identify causation.

Reviewer confidence: medium. Thesis family: hormuz-supply-magnitude.

Independent evidence
  • March 2026 oil market report · 2026-03-12
    IEA projected an 8mb/d March global supply decline, with offsets from other regions and emergency reserve releases; chokepoint throughput is not identical to net global supply lost.
  • Global LNG disruption · 2026-04-24
    Close to 20% of LNG supply was disrupted; LNG is a subset of natural gas. March global LNG production was 8% lower year over year after offsets elsewhere.

185-C15 · explanation · source blocks 67, 69, 75

Low-growth businesses can reward cheap entry.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

For a perpetuity with annual distributable cash C and required return r, value is C/r even at zero growth. Paying less raises the cash yield, conditional on persistence.

Assumptions: Cash flow persists; Distributions reach shareholders; No offsetting dilution or terminal loss

Positive distributable cash flow can produce a positive investor return without growth if the purchase price is low enough and the cash flow persists. The tobacco analogy does not prove alcohol demand or payout safety follows the same path.
