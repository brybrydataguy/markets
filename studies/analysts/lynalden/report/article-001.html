Report 001 · 2019-11-10

Introducing the First Top Twelve

Defensive macro posture and twelve value selections.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Evidence needed · 9Mixed · 13Not testable · 5Right · 2
The broad framework is explicitly uncertain, but cheap multiples and yields do not automatically establish adequate risk compensation. New disclosed return sensitivities show strong Southern Copper and eventual Booking results, weaker Scotiabank and TotalEnergies opportunity costs, and near-but-below-target Enbridge compounding. Invesco's dividend reduction and Russian investor-access disruption remain important counterexamples. Unspecified horizons and corporate actions limit precise grading; repeated risk language is not multiple independent recession forecasts.
Coverage. All numbered text blocks and image labels read. Material theses, forecasts and explanations are included; routine holdings lists, incidental statistics, company descriptions and boilerplate are excluded. Chart pixels were not inspected.
