Defensiveness from recession signals.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Right · 8Wrong · 5Mixed · 13Not testable · 8Evidence needed · 2
The article makes unusually testable bearish calls: later equity lows and a Q1 policy-rate peak both failed. The recession call also failed. Gold and tobacco later performed well, while BMY disappointed, illustrating why sector narratives cannot substitute for per-asset evidence. Custody and reserve-proof analysis was stronger than the inferred regulatory consensus or the claim that a chain halt destroys proof-of-stake history.
Coverage. All article text blocks were read. Ledger covers material macro, investment, and causal propositions; incidental descriptive statistics, boilerplate, and unreasoned position lists are excluded. Image labels were read, not image pixels.
