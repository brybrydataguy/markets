Bills resemble money.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Approximate percentage price change equals -modified duration times yield change. Lower duration therefore means smaller interest-rate price exposure, all else equal.

Assumptions: Same default/liquidity risk; Small parallel yield shift; Shorter modified duration

Narrow price-risk statement follows from bond mathematics at equal credit quality. This is a deductive explanation, not empirical evidence that any bill is identical to cash.
