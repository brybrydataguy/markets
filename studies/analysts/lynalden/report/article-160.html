160-C27 · explanation · source blocks 77, 78, 95

Tariff revenue

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Static revenue 1.45 times $500 bn = $725 bn; actual revenue = rate times realized taxable customs value, not the fixed historical base.

Assumptions: Ad valorem rate 1.45; Pre-response taxable imports $500bn; Actual exemptions and quantity changes alter taxable base

The static calculation is an upper counterfactual with unchanged taxable import value; exemptions and quantity responses reduce the base. It should not be treated as a realized $700 bn tax receipt forecast.
