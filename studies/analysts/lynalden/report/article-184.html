Private Credit, Software, and Energy

Private credit, banks and energy stress.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Right · 7Mixed · 6Evidence needed · 4Not testable · 5Wrong · 1
This report updates the Iran view and makes useful distinctions between bank deposits and patient investment capital. The central banking conclusion has partial independent support, but exposure ratios are not sufficient stress tests and the treatment of undrawn credit as not yet risky is incorrect. Adobe’s reported business growth is stronger evidence than its claimed cheapness.

184-C01 · explanation · source blocks 9, 10, 11

Dollar debts create compulsory crisis demand.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

The borrower must convert resources into dollars or default. Aggregate exchange-rate effects additionally depend on other demand and supply.

Assumptions: Dollar obligation remains payable; Borrower lacks sufficient dollar cash; No refinancing or renegotiation

Fixed dollar obligations require dollar settlement unless renegotiated. Falling revenues can force borrowers to acquire dollars or liquidate assets. This bounded mechanism does not imply that the exchange rate always rises, since other flows can dominate.

184-C02 · explanation · source blocks 13, 25

Costlier energy squeezes other spending.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Non-energy budget equals total budget less price times energy quantity; increasing energy price reduces that residual.

Assumptions: Budget fixed; Energy quantity unchanged

For a household or firm with a fixed nominal budget, higher expenditure on an unchanged energy quantity leaves less for other uses. Producer income, borrowing, fiscal transfers and substitution prevent this partial-equilibrium identity from proving an equal aggregate-demand loss.

184-C14 · explanation · source blocks 94, 109, 113, 125

Investor capital absorbs losses before bank lenders.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsBank claim is senior to fund equity
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

If asset value falls but remains above senior debt, residual equity bears the decline. Senior principal becomes impaired once asset value falls below its claim.

Assumptions: Single asset pool; Senior bank debt legally enforceable; Equity junior; No off-balance-sheet senior claims

The priority mechanism holds for a solvent fund’s ordinary senior secured borrowing with adequate equity/subordination. It does not guarantee collateral valuation, enforceability or liquidity under stress, nor apply uniformly to every nonbank structure.
