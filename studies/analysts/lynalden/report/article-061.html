2021 Global Opportunities Report

Country valuations and external balance sheets.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Not testable · 5Too early · 1Wrong · 3Mixed · 12Evidence needed · 1Right · 3
The five-year country recommendations are now testable in horizon, but their risk-adjustment rule is unspecified. USD proxies show a highly uneven record: China and Indonesia lost value; Russia suffered an investment-access failure; Taiwan and Poland strongly gained. The supposedly low-ranked US beat most favored countries. The sound balance-sheet distinction between gross debt and net assets does not by itself predict equity returns. NIIP explanations need valuation effects as well as current-account flows.

061-C19 · explanation · source blocks 26

Dollar debt amplifies stress

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Local debt service = e*D; its increase is D*change(e), reducing cash available after debt service.

Assumptions: Fixed unhedged dollar debt service D; exchange rate e in local currency per dollar rises; local revenues held fixed.

The narrow balance-sheet mechanism is arithmetic. It does not mean every dollar rally inevitably causes a crisis, since hedges, reserves and export revenues can offset exposure.

Direction: Supported for the defined proposition.
