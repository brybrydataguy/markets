144-C02 · explanation · source blocks 14

TGA spending adds reserves.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Fed debits TGA by X and credits recipient bank reserves by X; bank credits recipient deposit by X. Therefore TGA decrease X corresponds to reserve increase X under these assumptions.

Assumptions: Treasury pays a recipient at a commercial bank; No offsetting Fed transaction; Other Fed liabilities fixed

Narrow balance-sheet transfer is valid when Treasury spends to commercial-bank deposit holders and all other Federal Reserve balance-sheet items are fixed.
