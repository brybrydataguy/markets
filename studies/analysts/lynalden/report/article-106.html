Report 106 · 2023-03-19

Deep Dive: A Trend Change

Bank stress changes liquidity interpretation.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Wrong · 5Right · 10Mixed · 13Not testable · 5Evidence needed · 1Too early · 1
The banking diagnosis improves substantially and acknowledges the prior blind spot. Gold, Bitcoin and Canadian producer directions work over their stated periods. The key liquidity extrapolation fails: emergency lending did not end QT, and balance-sheet runoff resumed. Private support for First Republic was not proof of viability. Treat the later successful asset outcomes separately from the erroneous policy path used to justify them.
Coverage. All article text blocks were read. Ledger covers material macro, investment, and causal propositions; incidental descriptive statistics, boilerplate, and unreasoned position lists are excluded. Image labels were read, not image pixels.
