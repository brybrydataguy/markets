Economic Update

Income normalization, Fed policy and energy allocation.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Right · 8Evidence needed · 3Not testable · 8Mixed · 5Wrong · 2Too early · 2
The strongest resolved calls are the precise May core-PCE range and taper-before-hikes sequence, plus the three-year energy allocation. The policy interpretation contains a substantive error: flexible inflation averaging did not obligate the Fed to compensate the prior decade, and the earlier target was already symmetric. Separate those institutional inaccuracies from the successful near-term policy patience forecast. Long-decade forecasts remain open; causal oil and speculation arguments are less well identified.
Coverage. Read all blocks and image labels, including valuation and portfolio sections. Excluded scheduling, disclaimers and position lists without theses. Separate near-term measured predictions, historical explanations and decade-long forecasts.

058-C02 · explanation · source blocks 8, 23

Low bases amplify growth.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

g = current/prior - 1; reducing the positive prior level increases g.

Assumptions: Positive current and prior-period levels; current level held fixed.

This is arithmetic, not a separate macro forecast. It explains why a low comparison period can amplify a year-over-year reading even with unchanged current activity.
