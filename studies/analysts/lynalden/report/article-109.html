Energy Sector Update: A Look at Balance Sheets

Balance-sheet quality within three timeframes.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Mixed · 20Right · 6Wrong · 2Not testable · 4Too early · 1
The clearest successful forecasts are Bitcoin’s new high before end-2025 and Southwestern’s acquisition. The adopted late-2023 rate-cut expectation and stagflationary recession base case fail. The author now explicitly identifies the bill/RRP exception that would invalidate the QE-necessity narrative, an important improvement. Producer balance-sheet analysis is stronger than the rigid macro cycle timing.
Coverage. All article text blocks were read. Ledger covers material macro, investment, and causal propositions; incidental descriptive statistics, boilerplate, and unreasoned position lists are excluded. Image labels were read, not image pixels.
