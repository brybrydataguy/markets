180-C01 · explanation · source blocks 9, 10, 14

Overnight usage is not cumulative.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Settlement adds reserves; maturity removes the same principal. Replacing a matured transaction maintains outstanding funding rather than accumulating it.

Assumptions: Principal repaid on maturity; Rollovers separately recorded

Repayment reverses each overnight reserve addition unless replaced. Daily gross transactions cannot be summed as permanent stimulus. The reported $31bn net facility absorption is a level relative to neither facility, not necessarily the change in reserves from the previous day.

180-C22 · explanation · source blocks 145, 146, 147, 158

Dollar weakness relieves debt.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Local-currency debt equals the dollar liability multiplied by the exchange rate in local currency per dollar. Dollar depreciation reduces that product.

Assumptions: Fixed dollar liability; Local-currency income; No offsetting hedge or dollar asset

For an unhedged borrower with local-currency income and fixed dollar debt, a weaker dollar reduces the local-currency burden. This is a conditional balance-sheet mechanism, not a universal emerging-market stock forecast. Export revenues, hedges and local policy can change the exposure.

Analytical tools and framework

  • SOFR-IORB and reserve balances: Separate policy implementation from risk-asset returns (image not visually inspected)
  • GDPNow component decomposition: Inspect trade distortions and later national-account adjustments (image not visually inspected)
  • Uranium supply-demand projections: Separate spot prices, contracts and long construction cycles (image not visually inspected)
  • MVRV and Bitcoin trend bands: Historical heuristics, not guaranteed floors (image not visually inspected)
  • Dollar and emerging-market relative returns: Separate translation effects, local fundamentals and investor returns (image not visually inspected)
  • Expected-value scenarios: Make probabilities explicit without treating illustrative odds as calibrated (image not visually inspected)
