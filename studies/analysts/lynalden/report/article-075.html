The report increasingly separated long-term Bitcoin optimism from tactical caution. Macro risk warnings were useful, while China optimism failed. HDFC remediation is independently confirmed, but decade investment outcomes remain open. The Russia pairing exposes a major blind spot: national balance-sheet strength does not guarantee foreign shareholder access.
