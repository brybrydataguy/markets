Value Stock Update: Banks and Pipelines

Rate timing, bank risk and dividend value.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Mixed · 10Right · 8Conditional · 2Wrong · 4Too early · 3Not testable · 4
The 2023 rate-cut base case and preferred 3.5% ten-year yield peak were premature. The bank analysis correctly contrasts credit exposures with 2008 but underweights funding and duration risk exposed in 2023. Bitcoin and broad energy gained over the first three-year endpoint; EPD's five-year view is still open. HDFC merger timing and TROW's 2022 earnings warning worked. Address-based custody inferences and reported Mt. Gox timing need more care than the narrative gives them.
Coverage. All article text blocks were read. Ledger covers material macro, investment, and causal propositions; incidental descriptive statistics, boilerplate, and unreasoned position lists are excluded. Image labels were read, not image pixels.
