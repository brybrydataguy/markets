The two central investment forecasts disappoint: MARA lost 71.47% over the stated year, and Amazon remained far below its split-adjusted $250 target at end 2023. MARA's output did grow, but deployment delays activated risks the author explicitly identified and prevented the projected monthly production. Amazon's regulatory caveat is preserved; filing a lawsuit is distinct from executing a breakup. The comparative market-cap forecast needs share-count evidence, not a substitution of share-price returns.
