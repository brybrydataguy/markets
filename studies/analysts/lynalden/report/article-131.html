131-C09 · explanation · source blocks 53, 58, 64

Existing holders set marginal supply.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Available sell flow at price p equals newly issued coins offered plus existing inventory offered; issuance is therefore not an upper bound on market supply.

Assumptions: Existing coins can be transferred; Positive holder willingness to sell at some prices

New issuance is only one source of tradable coins. The stock can transact repeatedly; price clears willingness to buy and sell, so comparing ETF demand only with mined supply is incomplete.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: Supported for the defined proposition.

Timing: No fixed deadline stated.

Mechanism: Narrow deductive identity only; no empirical forecasting credit.

Reviewer confidence: high. Thesis family: bitcoin-liquid-supply.

Independent evidence

This explanation is assessed through its stated derivation, not an observed forecast result.
