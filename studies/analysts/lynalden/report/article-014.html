014-C12 · empirical · source blocks 38

Basel III newly makes gold Tier 1

Wrong
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted

This confuses asset risk weights with bank capital. Basel III Tier 1 consists of qualifying loss-absorbing capital; allocated bullion could already receive zero credit-risk weight under Basel II in 2006. The asserted new status cannot explain a change from 2008.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: See reason; no return inferred.

Timing: No fixed endpoint supplied.

Mechanism: Regulatory premise is incorrect; price outcome cannot validate it.

Reviewer confidence: high. Thesis family: gold-basel-tier1-error.

Independent evidence
  • Basel II comprehensive framework, footnote 32 · 2006-06-30
    Gold bullion held in own vaults or allocated and backed by bullion liabilities could already be treated as cash with a zero credit-risk weight at national discretion. This predates the 2008 crisis.
  • Basel III regulatory framework, paragraphs 49-53 · 2011-06-01
    Tier 1 comprises qualifying loss-absorbing capital instruments; Common Equity Tier 1 includes common shares, retained earnings and qualifying reserves. Gold asset holdings are not newly designated Tier 1 capital.
