Liquidity, inflation and banks.

Not testable · 18Wrong · 8Mixed · 14Right · 11Conditional · 1Too early · 1Evidence needed · 4
Several calendar forecasts are sharply testable: May CPI above 4.5% and infrastructure enactment were right, while a summer inflation peak and no substantial 2022 rate hikes were wrong. The GBTC discussion identifies the discount mechanism but mistakenly calls a lockup-dependent premium trade risk-free. Bank risks require more care: rising rates can produce acute losses, not merely slow margin erosion.
Coverage. All sections read, including portfolio rotations, closed-end trust mechanics, four bank-risk categories and conditional macro scenarios. Short current COVID counts and individual balance-sheet statistics are incidental; all material investment and causal conclusions are retained. The article contains explicit inflation and rate deadlines that are scored separately.

Sizing limits harm.

Right
HorizonNot specified

Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Portfolio simple return contribution equals w times bitcoin return; a 50% loss at 5% weight reduces portfolio value by 2.5%.

Assumptions: Unleveraged bitcoin weight w; Other assets unchanged; No rebalancing during loss

With no leverage and fixed weights, an asset loss contributes proportionally less to a diversified portfolio. Psychological comfort and overall suitability are not mechanically guaranteed.
