Energy Sector Checkup

Liquidity improves; energy serves as portfolio insurance.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Right · 9Mixed · 11Not testable · 11Evidence needed · 8Too early · 2
Near-term liquidity and asset appreciation worked, and the gold breakout and EPD distribution calls were supported. The energy case needs a distinction between portfolio insurance and a forecast that drilling constraints will cap production: EIA evidence shows substantial productivity gains. Money aggregates require consolidation rather than treating near-money instruments as interchangeable. Binance enforcement verifies compliance oversight, not solvency. The 3-5 year energy horizon and the full 2024-2026 gold interval remain open.

Real slowdown

Mixed
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Real growth = (1+g)/(1+p)-1, negative if g<p. CPI is not necessarily the matching p.

Assumptions: Nominal quantity index growth g; Matching sector price growth p

Growth below an appropriate price deflator implies falling real volume. Applying general CPI indiscriminately to construction, capital orders, exports and imports can misclassify real growth because sector prices differ.
