168-C13 · explanation · source blocks 69, 85

Premium equity issuance can increase Bitcoin per share.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

New net assets per share exceed old net assets per share when proceeds per new share exceed the prior net asset value per share.

Assumptions: Issue price above existing net assets per share; Net proceeds invested without offsetting losses; Senior claims and transaction costs included

Issuing shares above net asset value and buying the same underlying asset can increase assets per share after costs. Enterprise-value-to-gross-Bitcoin mNAV is not identical to common-equity-to-net-assets NAV and must be adjusted for senior claims. Share-count dilution and per-share accretion can occur together.
