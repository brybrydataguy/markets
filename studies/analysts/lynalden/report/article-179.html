179-C02 · explanation · source blocks 23

Overnight repo usage is not cumulative stimulus.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

The reserve addition at settlement is offset by repayment at maturity; rolling the principal restores the outstanding stock rather than adding the daily principal cumulatively.

Assumptions: Overnight repo principal is repaid at maturity; No default or extension unless separately recorded

A maturing overnight transaction reverses unless replaced. Summing each day’s gross rollovers as new permanent liquidity double-counts the same financing. Changes in outstanding balances, maturities and other Fed liabilities are the relevant reserve-accounting quantities.

Analytical tools and framework

  • Repo and reverse-repo facilities: Compare outstanding stocks and daily changes; include all eligible collateral (image not visually inspected)
  • SOFR minus IORB: Separate calendar spikes from persistent funding pressure (image not visually inspected)
  • Continental oil production: Expose regional contributions while checking offsetting countries (image not visually inspected)
  • Commodity stock/flow comparison: Combine inventories with storage costs and market structure (image not visually inspected)
  • FAST Graphs: energy equities: Separate operating resilience from entry valuation (image not visually inspected)
