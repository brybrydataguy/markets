Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted

Darden and Carnival show the concrete channel: fixed cash obligations persisted while revenue collapsed, forcing financing and payout cuts. This supports amplification, not the claim debt alone caused the pandemic recession.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: Resolved as stated.

Timing: No fixed endpoint supplied.

Mechanism: Outcome evidence does not independently identify causation.

Reviewer confidence: medium. Thesis family: covid-financial-amplification.

Independent evidence

    Pandemic response included full revolver draw, $270 million term loan, equity offering and dividend suspension. Q4 sales declined 43%; company survived but changed its financing structure.
