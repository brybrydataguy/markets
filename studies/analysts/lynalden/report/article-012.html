012-C06 · explanation · source blocks 23

Cash-secured puts reduce downside and cap upside

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsSame capital comparison; puts remain fully cash secured
Author modalityasserted

For the same expiry and an out-of-the-money strike, cash plus a short put has lower maximum loss than immediate stock ownership at the higher spot price; premium caps the upside. This is payoff arithmetic, not assurance of attractive realized risk-adjusted returns.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: Resolved as stated.

Timing: No fixed endpoint supplied.

Mechanism: Payoff identity excluding fees, tax and cash yield.

Reviewer confidence: medium. Thesis family: cash-secured-put-risk.

Independent evidence
  • Options assignment FAQ
    American-style exercise is the holder's decision before expiration. At expiration, exercise-by-exception thresholds and contrary instructions apply; going in the money does not itself compel early assignment.
