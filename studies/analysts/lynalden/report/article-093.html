Egypt tourism resilience is contradicted by aggregate data. The Brazil rate-peak and relative-performance observations have support, while its election-discount and long-run valuation claims are less testable. The Bitcoin three-year result worked; oil spot weakness and favorable midstream returns demonstrate why those exposures must be separated. Technical PoS concerns identify real bootstrap and concentration issues but overgeneralize temporary outages and comparative decentralization. The report candidly acknowledges FedEx, gold and Fed-timing disappointments, and discloses an August Zoom sale omitted from the prior publication, limiting reconstructed trade timing.

Independent evidence
  • Weak subjectivity
    Nodes joining or returning after long absence need a recent trusted checkpoint; continuously participating nodes retain finalized state. Multiple independent checkpoint sources are possible.
