Fiscal transfers explain income divergence

Mixed
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted

Transfers supported income, but declaring stimulus inactive omits ongoing programs, delayed payments and accumulated savings. A flow slowdown is not the elimination of all fiscal support.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: See reason; no return inferred.

Timing: No fixed endpoint supplied.

Mechanism: Outcome evidence does not independently identify causation.

Reviewer confidence: medium. Thesis family: fiscal-income-buffer.

Independent evidence
  • May 2020 personal income and outlays · 2020-06-26
    Personal income fell 4.2% in May after revised 10.8% growth in April. Declining recovery payments explained much of the decrease, partly offset by unemployment benefits. Spending rose 8.2%.
  • CARES and Consolidated Appropriations Acts
    The subsequent broad relief act passed Congress December 21 and was signed December 27, 2020, after the election rather than during summer.
