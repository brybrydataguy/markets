189-C10 · explanation · source blocks 69

Buybacks increase ownership per remaining share.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Ownership fraction equals one divided by shares outstanding; EPS equals earnings divided by that same share count.

Assumptions: Net shares decline after dilution; Aggregate earnings held constant

If net share count falls, each remaining share represents a larger fraction of the company. EPS rises mechanically only holding aggregate earnings constant, and value creation additionally depends on the repurchase price.

Operating cash flows reduce external-capital dependence.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

An internally funded purchase uses retained cash rather than new debt or equity, reducing external financing required by that amount.

Assumptions: Positive cash remains after required expenditures; Treasury purchases do not exceed retained available cash

Retained operating cash can fund treasury purchases without issuing securities, provided acquisition costs, debt service, taxes and reinvestment are covered. That financing identity does not establish positive investor returns or stable cash flows.
