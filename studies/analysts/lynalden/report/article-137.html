137-C12 · explanation · source blocks 66, 106

Curve fits cannot determine bitcoin demand.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Infinitely many functions fit the same finite observations yet diverge afterward. Historical fit alone therefore does not uniquely determine future prices.

Assumptions: Only a finite historical set of price observations is known; No independently established structural demand restriction

Extrapolating past prices is not an identified demand model. Model fit alone cannot establish invariant future adoption or causal law; the earlier 2020 target is historical, not a fresh forecast.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence
