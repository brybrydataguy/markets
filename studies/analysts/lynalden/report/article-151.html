151-C09 · explanation · source blocks 35

Wages lagging money lose relative share.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

W/M falls when(1+g W)/(1+g M)<1. Real wage W/CPI is a separate ratio.

Assumptions: Wage W and money stock M measured consistently; W grows slower than M; No claim that M equals consumer price index

Relative-to-money-supply dilution is mathematically distinct from loss of consumer purchasing power, which depends on prices.
