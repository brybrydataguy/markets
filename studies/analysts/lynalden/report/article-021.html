Berkshire Hathaway (BRK.B) Deep Dive Analysis

Financial-sector value depends on solvency, fiscal support and rates.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Right · 7Mixed · 6Not testable · 3Evidence needed · 3Wrong · 2Too early · 2
The delayed job recovery, bank earnings decline and fiscal-reflation direction were supported. The expected Treasury yield-control regime failed. Reserve creation is repeatedly confused with bank recapitalization, a material mechanistic error. Berkshire succession risk matched an observed decline, while the post-succession restructuring thesis remains early.

021-C09 · explanation · source blocks 49, 56, 59, 61

QE recapitalizes banks through reserves

Wrong
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted

A market-value exchange of securities for reserves improves liquidity but does not automatically increase equity capital. If the seller is a nonbank, the bank gains a reserve asset and matching deposit liability. Loss absorption requires capital, profits, guarantees or direct recapitalization, not merely a larger reserve balance.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: See reason; no return inferred.

Timing: No fixed endpoint supplied.

Mechanism: Outcome evidence does not independently identify causation.

Reviewer confidence: high. Thesis family: qe-reserves-versus-capital.

Independent evidence
  • How money is created · 2019-10-01
    Reserves are electronic central-bank settlement money. Capital is a separate resource that absorbs loan-default losses; both liquidity and capital constraints apply to banks.
