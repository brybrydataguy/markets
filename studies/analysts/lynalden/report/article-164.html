164-C13 · explanation · source blocks 106, 107, 109

Exchange balances do not imply a squeeze.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

The measured exchange balance falls despite no sale, new buyer or change in total coins; therefore the balance change is not sufficient evidence of a squeeze.

Assumptions: Coins transfer between custody classifications; Owner and total supply unchanged

A transfer from an exchange trading account to institutional custody can change a labeled balance without changing beneficial ownership or total supply. Thus a lower exchange count alone cannot establish a shortage or short squeeze. This is a useful correction to a common on-chain inference.
