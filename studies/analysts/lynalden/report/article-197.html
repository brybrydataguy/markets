197-C13 · explanation · source blocks 84, 85, 87, 88, 90, 105

Higher discount rates challenge expensive equities.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Each positive future cash flow is divided by a larger discount factor, reducing the sum of present values.

Assumptions: Expected future cash flows unchanged; Required discount rate rises

For unchanged expected cash flows, a higher required return lowers present value. The implication is conditional: stronger earnings expectations can offset the discount-rate effect, so this mechanism does not automatically predict every high-multiple stock will fall.

197-C21 · explanation · source blocks 115

Geographic diversification can reduce volatility.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Portfolio variance includes covariance terms; correlations below one reduce variance relative to a perfectly correlated combination of the same component volatilities.

Assumptions: Portfolio weights fixed; Asset return correlations below one

With imperfectly correlated holdings, diversification can reduce variance relative to concentrated exposure at comparable weights. The benefit is conditional and can shrink during common shocks; geographic labels alone do not ensure different economic risks.
