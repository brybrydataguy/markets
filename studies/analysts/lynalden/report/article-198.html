198-C06 · explanation · source blocks 53, 54, 55

Cash-rich fixed-rate borrowers gain interest income.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Net interest-income change equals cash balance times yield increase, while debt interest is unchanged.

Assumptions: Cash yield reprices upward; Debt payment is fixed; Balances unchanged

For unchanged cash balances earning a floating rate and unchanged fixed-rate debt payments, a rate increase raises nominal net interest income. This validates the personal example but cannot be generalized to the whole economy without measuring other households and firms.

At a fixed distribution, a higher required equity yield mathematically implies a lower price. Actual valuation also depends on expected distribution growth, risk and taxes; Treasury yield alone does not dictate EPD’s spread.
