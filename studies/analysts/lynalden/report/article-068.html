Financial Sector Update

Inflation persistence, crypto governance, and discounted banks.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Evidence needed · 13Mixed · 20Right · 7Not testable · 11Wrong · 3Too early · 1
Persistent inflation and rent pressure were useful calls. Sberbank exposed the difference between cheap fundamentals and investable foreign-holder rights; DBS and CNQ fared well. Treasury-heavy bank portfolios were incorrectly treated as broadly safe. Crypto analysis correctly identified correlation and legal distinctions, but sometimes converted complex securities, governance and tax questions into overly categorical claims.
Coverage. Read all blocks 0-199 and image labels. Material epidemiology, inflation, currency, yield, uranium, crypto/legal, bank and valuation theses included. Anecdotes are not promoted into representative statistics; routine holdings and incidental levels excluded.
