The Boom in AI Agents

Liquidity, bitcoin, airlines and AI agents.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Mixed · 6Not testable · 6Evidence needed · 4Right · 6Too early · 3
The bounded liquidity call was directionally useful but its smooth path failed in April. The yuan direction held through the cutoff, and the agent-security concern has independent support. Bitcoin and memory forecasts remain open. The most important weaknesses are vague horizons and causal leaps from persistent agent memory to RAM demand, and from gold trade to reported GDP.
Coverage. Full numbered text and image inventory read. All material theses across macro, digital assets, investment analysis and concluding sections considered. Position listings and incidental descriptive statistics are excluded unless they support a thesis.

181-C05 · explanation · source blocks 22

Weaker dollars ease unhedged debt burdens.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Local debt service equals dollar debt service multiplied by local currency units per dollar. A fall in that exchange rate lowers the burden.

Assumptions: Dollar debt service fixed; Domestic revenue and hedges unchanged

For a fixed dollar liability and domestic-currency revenue, fewer local currency units per dollar reduce the local cost of service. The qualifier matters: hedges, dollar revenues and other policy changes can offset this channel.

Portfolio variance is w²σ²+(1-w)²σ²+2w(1-w)ρσ², below σ² for 0<w<1 and ρ<1.
