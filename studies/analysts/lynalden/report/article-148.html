Emerging Markets: Catalyst, but Follow Through?

Debt-ceiling liquidity and emerging-market catalysts.

The Microsoft vote, bitcoin breakout and twelve-month direction were concrete successes. Debt-ceiling cash drainage also occurred, including under the supposedly less contentious Trump scenario. Emerging-market analysis was appropriately conditional but does not justify a precise hit rate; illustrative JD probabilities must never be mistaken for forecasts.
Coverage. All sections read including macro distribution, digital assets, Microsoft proposal, EM countries and gold. JD scenario numbers are explicitly illustrative and not attributed as her probability forecast. Prior 2023 and September 2024 recaps are linked families rather than new calls.

Assumptions: Dollar debt service D fixed; Exchange ratee local units per dollar; Local revenue fixed; no hedging or dollar income
