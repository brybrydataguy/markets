147-C12 · explanation · source blocks 49, 50, 61

Premium shorting can fail.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Short profit=P 0-Pt. NAV premium alone imposes no bound on Pt, so losses need not wait for or end with convergence.

Assumptions: One share shorted at P0; No binding future price cap; Ignore borrow fees, which only worsen losses

An overpriced asset can appreciate before converging; no finite bound on losses follows from NAV premium alone. This does not establish a particular fund failure.

Diversification limits stock mistakes.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Its contribution to portfolio return is w*r; reducing w reduces absolute loss contribution for r<0.

Assumptions: Unlevered portfolio; Other holdings unchanged; Position has initial weight w and return r

A position loss contributes its portfolio weight times its return, holding others fixed. This does not protect against correlated market losses.

147-C27 · explanation · source blocks 106

Long bonds retain duration risk.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

P=sum(CFt/(1+y)^t); d P/dy=-sum(t*CFt/(1+y)^(t+1))<0.

Assumptions: Positive fixed cash flows CFt; Positive discount factor1+y; No default or embedded options
