Deep Dive: A Barbell Approach

Defensive income alongside selective risk.

Mixed · 14Right · 11Not testable · 5Conditional · 1Too early · 8Evidence needed · 1
The report improves its treatment of TGA and RRP offsets and correctly anticipates debt-limit resolution and a credit-spread widening episode. The first-half sideways case understated the rally. Its banking reassurance still overlooks duration and deposit-flight risk. Bitcoin gains over three years are clear; HDB and ITUB illustrate sharply different equity outcomes. Ordinals and Nostr mechanisms are substantially sound, with overstated permanence/censorship implications and unverified adoption diagnostics.
Coverage. All article text blocks were read. Ledger covers material macro, investment, and causal propositions; incidental descriptive statistics, boilerplate, and unreasoned position lists are excluded. Image labels were read, not image pixels.
