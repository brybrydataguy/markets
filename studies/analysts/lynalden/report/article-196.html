Right

ConditionsNot specified

196-C09 · explanation · source blocks 86, 87, 88

Bill-funded buybacks remove private duration.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Private holders exchange longer cash-flow duration for shorter cash-flow duration; aggregate duration exposure declines relative to the counterfactual.

Assumptions: Same dollar funding amount; Long bonds repurchased and replaced by much shorter bills

With coupon auction sizes unchanged and bills serving as the marginal funding instrument, purchasing long bonds reduces private duration relative to otherwise identical issuance. This accounting result does not establish the size of the yield response or a guaranteed increase in broad money.

196-C13 · explanation · source blocks 115

Yield caps can require unlimited bond purchases.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

If offered supply exceeds private demand at the floor, the authority must absorb the residual to maintain it; a predetermined purchase cap could permit the price to fall.

Assumptions: Authority commits to a fixed bond price floor; Eligible sellers may tender any quantity under program rules

A credible fixed price floor requires the authority to buy whatever eligible supply arrives at that price, subject to implementation terms. This defines a yield-cap commitment; it does not show that the 2026 Treasury buyback program is such a commitment.
