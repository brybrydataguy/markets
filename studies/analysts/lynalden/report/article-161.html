161-C04 · explanation · source blocks 19, 21

Treasury cash draws support reserves.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

The Fed debits TGA and credits recipient banks’ reserve balances by the same amount.

Assumptions: Other Fed balance-sheet entries fixed; Treasury payment settles through commercial banks

Holding other Fed liabilities and assets constant, a Treasury payment transfers balances from TGA to bank reserves. A simultaneous tax receipt or debt sale can offset this, so the direction is conditional accounting rather than a guaranteed asset-price effect.

161-C08 · explanation · source blocks 27, 28, 30, 145

Dollar weakness eases foreign debt burdens.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Local debt burden equals dollar debt times local-currency units per dollar; a lower exchange rate reduces the burden.

Assumptions: Debt fixed in dollars; Local-currency resources unchanged; No offsetting hedge

For unchanged dollar liabilities and local-currency resources, dollar depreciation reduces the local-currency debt burden. The total effect also depends on export prices, hedges and financing access. Calling every dollar decline an increase in global liquidity is broader than this bounded mechanism.
