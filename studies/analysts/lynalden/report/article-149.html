Alphabet Deep Dive Analysis

Election liquidity and Alphabet resilience.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Wrong · 1Right · 10Mixed · 7Evidence needed · 5Not testable · 7Too early · 2
The confident post-election no-TGA-drain update was contradicted. QT timing, tax-rate extension, regulatory easing and Alphabet resilience were supported. Alphabet avoided the feared breakup, but attributing that result to political leniency would be wrong. AI capital spending remains a major return-on-investment question.
Coverage. Read every section and heatmap inventory. Covers fiscal outlook, bonds/mortgages, Bitcoin governance and regulation, Alphabet valuation, competition, capital allocation and antitrust. Historical breakout recap is linked to prior forecasts, not counted anew.

149-C13 · explanation · source blocks 70

Operators choose software updates.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

A separately published client changes only machines that install it; publication does not mutate the local validation program.

Assumptions: Operator controls executable and configuration; No automatic update or compromise; Old client can continue executing

For a self-managed node without automatic updating, publishing a new executable cannot itself replace the installed rules. Economic coordination may still pressure a choice.
