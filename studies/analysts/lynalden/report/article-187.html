Fiscal Re-Acceleration

Fiscal momentum, bank resilience and DeFi risk.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Right · 9Wrong · 1Evidence needed · 4Not testable · 4Mixed · 9
The fiscal reacceleration call has a measurable success, and the FIMA explanation correctly identifies a funding backstop. The jet-fuel denominator is wrong. DeFi emergency control validates a centralization concern, while Lido’s falling market share challenges a simple winner-take-all story. Bank results are constructive but mixed across securities.
Coverage. Full numbered text and image inventory read. All material theses across macro, digital assets, investment analysis and concluding sections considered. Position listings and incidental descriptive statistics are excluded unless they support a thesis.

187-C06 · explanation · source blocks 44

Treasury cash reductions release reserves.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

A fall in the Treasury deposit liability is offset by a rise in bank reserve liabilities; the Fed balance sheet remains balanced.

Assumptions: Treasury spends from its Fed account; Other Fed assets and liabilities held constant

With other Fed balance-sheet entries unchanged, a reduction in the Treasury’s Fed deposit increases another liability, usually bank reserves when Treasury spends. Longer issuance maturity is a separate price/duration choice.
