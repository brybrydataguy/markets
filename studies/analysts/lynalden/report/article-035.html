Facebook (FB) Deep Dive Analysis

Facebook value and a fiscal trading framework.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Mixed · 12Wrong · 3Evidence needed · 5Not testable · 5Right · 3Too early · 1
Facebook was a successful long-window selection and its conservative initial EPS growth was surpassed. The analysis usefully identified privacy sensitivity, but its assertion that buybacks had not begun was wrong. The comparison also underestimated Cadence growth. Fiscal timing and the proposed technical entry rule remain less auditable than the company forecast.
Coverage. All numbered text blocks and image labels read. Material theses, forecasts and explanations are included; routine holdings lists, incidental statistics, company descriptions and boilerplate are excluded. Chart pixels were not inspected.
