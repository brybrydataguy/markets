193-C01 · explanation · source blocks 9, 11, 14, 15

Depleted inventories reduce the energy buffer.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Available buffer equals current stock minus minimum stock; prior drawdowns reduce that quantity.

Assumptions: Same minimum operating inventory; No offsetting supply or demand change

For a fixed minimum operating stock and unchanged supply/demand, a lower inventory leaves less available drawdown capacity. This supports vulnerability, but the amount of future shortage also depends on rerouting, production and demand response.

193-C05 · explanation · source blocks 40, 43, 45

Japanese cash suffers under negative real rates.

Mixed
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Real gross return equals nominal gross return divided by the price-level growth factor; it is below one under these assumptions.

Assumptions: Nominal after-tax return below relevant inflation; Same measurement interval

A nominal return below inflation reduces purchasing power, but equities, foreign assets and hard money are not guaranteed hedges over every horizon. A weakening currency and rising yields alone do not prove capital flight or that all movements are deliberately controlled.
