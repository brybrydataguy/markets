Report 089 · 2022-07-24

Dividend Stock Update: Banks, Healthcare, and Industrials

Policy ceilings and dividend durability.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Mixed · 12Wrong · 3Right · 11Not testable · 10Too early · 2
The near-term rate ceiling and two-year yield peak were wrong, as was the no-reform outlook for drug pricing. The Merge timing and immediate ETH decline worked, though market co-movement limits causal attribution. The first three-year bullish equity and Bitcoin directions were positive, while five-year defensive income claims remain open. Bank balance-sheet analysis needs funding and duration stress, and Fedi development claims require clear release milestones and conflict disclosure.
Coverage. All article text blocks were read. Ledger covers material macro, investment, and causal propositions; incidental descriptive statistics, boilerplate, and unreasoned position lists are excluded. Image labels were read, not image pixels.
