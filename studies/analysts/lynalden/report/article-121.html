Deep Dive: Treasury Inflation Protected Securities

TIPS entry, fiscal transmission, and energy insurance.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Mixed · 18Evidence needed · 9Not testable · 12Right · 9Wrong · 2Too early · 2
The TIPS entry earned positive returns but did not beat nominal intermediate Treasuries over the two-year sensitivity. Broad equity caution proved too pessimistic. The report usefully concedes the earlier Fed-hawkishness miss, while the revised claim that higher rates are net inflationary remains unproven. TIPS principal protection needs a par-value qualification. Energy-disruption risk materialized within the decade, without validating every intermediate geopolitical scenario.
