136-C19 · explanation · source blocks 144, 146

Supply scarcity alone cannot establish value.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

A fixed supply permits multiple market-clearing prices for different demand schedules, including zero demand. Scarcity alone cannot determine positive value.

Assumptions: Asset supply is fixed at S>0; Demand may be zero or vary independently

A supply rule does not create demand. The stronger claim linking all frequent hard forks to centralization would require a governance measure and is not established.
