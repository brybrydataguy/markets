006-C20 · forecast · source blocks 132, 136

Enterprise should outperform expensive growth during selloff

Wrong
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsBroad market selloff
Author modalityshould

In the ensuing crash, the vendor distribution-adjusted proxy fell 51.94% for EPD versus 23.49% for QQQ from January 21 to March 23, 2020. QQQ is an evaluator-selected proxy for expensive growth, and March 23 is the common equity-crash endpoint. The very large relative loss contradicts the stated selloff-defense expectation despite resilient operating cash flow. No claim about all growth portfolios is made.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: Wrong for the disclosed growth proxy and ensuing selloff.

Timing: The broad selloff occurred within the forecast coming-months window.

Mechanism: Cash-flow durability did not prevent equity repricing and energy-sector liquidation.

Reviewer confidence: high. Thesis family: epd-growth-relative-defense.

Independent evidence
  • EPD daily historical prices and corporate actions
    January 21-March 23 adjusted-close proxy: -51.94%; no split events in the downloaded history.
  • QQQ daily historical prices and corporate actions
    Same-window adjusted-close proxy: -23.49%. QQQ is a growth-heavy Nasdaq 100 ETF proxy, not an author-specified benchmark.
