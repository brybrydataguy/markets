Markel (MKL) Deep Dive Analysis

Insurance value, recovery risks and Bitcoin upside.

The near-reporting-date Markel portfolio and book-value calls were borne out. Bitcoin also gained over the explicit two-year horizon, with severe drawdown. The PPP maturity language overlooks an already-enacted extension, and claims are again treated too literally as people. The preferred-stock ideas and tactical Tesla trade require holding-period discipline rather than retrospective cherry-picking.
