Position-size mitigation

Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Portfolio return contribution from asset i equals weight_i times return_i; reducing positive weight reduces absolute loss contribution for a fixed negative return.

Assumptions: Other holdings and returns fixed; No offsetting leverage increase

For an otherwise fixed portfolio and price shock, reducing the weight reduces that position contribution proportionally. This does not ensure the total portfolio becomes safer.
