Mixed
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted

The rule change can reduce capital costs of Treasury intermediation, but SLR measures Tier 1 capital relative to total leverage exposure. It does not let banks multiply a fixed reserve base in the way stated. The distinction matters when forecasting which balance sheet constraint binds.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: See reason; no return inferred.

Timing: No fixed endpoint supplied.

Mechanism: Outcome evidence does not independently identify causation.

Reviewer confidence: medium. Thesis family: slr-capital-versus-reserves.

Independent evidence
  • Final enhanced supplementary leverage rule · 2025-11-25
    Rule recalibrated leverage capital requirements for the largest banks, effective April 1, 2026 with optional January 1 adoption, to reduce disincentives to Treasury intermediation. This is a capital backstop, not a reserve requirement.
  • Supplementary leverage ratio denominator rule · 2014-09-03
    Supplementary leverage exposure includes on-balance-sheet assets and specified off-balance-sheet exposures; the capital standard is not a ratio of loans to reserve balances.
