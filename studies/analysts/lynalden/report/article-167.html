167-C24 · explanation · source blocks 142, 143

Small uncorrelated positions can reduce portfolio risk.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Portfolio variance equals the weighted variances plus twice weighted covariances; low covariance can offset added standalone variance.

Assumptions: Finite variances; Specified portfolio weights and covariances

Diversification can reduce variance when the new asset’s covariance with the portfolio is sufficiently low relative to its weight and volatility. Small size alone is insufficient, and correlations can rise during stress. The report appropriately treats airline-specific downside as a sizing concern.
