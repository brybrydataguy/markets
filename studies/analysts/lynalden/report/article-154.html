The dated Brazil rebound and bitcoin breakout worked, while equal-weight rotation and the shorter debt-ceiling dispute did not. Brazil current-account and TRUMP allocation errors are clear. The trade framework correctly highlights adjustment costs but overstates the necessity of US deficits for dollar creation. These errors should remain visible even where investment direction succeeded.
Coverage. All macro, tariff scenarios, digital assets, tax-loss examples and gold sections reviewed. Illustrative tariff arithmetic is separated from actual policy predictions; disputed wallet ownership is not treated as an established allegation.
