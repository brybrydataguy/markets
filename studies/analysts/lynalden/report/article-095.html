Treasury Market Deep Dive Analysis

Treasury capacity, energy and a tactical rally.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Wrong · 2Mixed · 19Right · 17Evidence needed · 2Not testable · 3
The two-week equity/Bitcoin rally call worked, and the three-year Bitcoin and midstream income directions were favorable. The 2023 recession forecast failed. The Treasury explanation usefully identifies intermediation constraints but omits real-rate and term-premium components, overstating what falling breakevens prove. RRP-funded bill demand and eventual buybacks were real policy channels. Energy capacity forecasts were better than domestic-gas price expectations; US gas supply expanded and prices fell sharply. First 19-month drilling-permit totals and the named bridge performance remain specific evidence gaps.
Coverage. All article text blocks were read. Ledger covers material macro, investment, and causal propositions; incidental descriptive statistics, boilerplate, and unreasoned position lists are excluded. Image labels were read, not image pixels.
