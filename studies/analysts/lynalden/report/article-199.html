Wrong

ConditionsNot specified

A relevant e SLR reform had already been finalized in November 2025 and taken effect in April 2026, though additional future changes remain possible.

199-C14 · explanation · source blocks 61, 64

Brazil combines high real rates with external buffers.

Mixed
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted

The central bank confirms a 13.75% policy rate. Official balance-of-payments data distinguish the goods surplus from the current-account deficit, correcting an earlier report’s conflation. Nominal policy rate minus trailing inflation is an ex-post shortcut, not the expected real return of a foreign investor; reserve adequacy needs short-term obligations and currency composition.
