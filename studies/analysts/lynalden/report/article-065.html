TGA refill drains liquidity

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Fed liabilities shift from bank reserves to Treasury deposit: change reserves = -change TGA for the isolated transaction.

Assumptions: Treasury issues securities paid from bank deposits; No simultaneous offsetting central-bank operation; Payment settles to Treasury account at Fed

With debt issuance paid from private-bank deposits, rebuilding Treasury cash transfers reserves to the Fed Treasury account. This accounting result does not determine yields or stock prices.
