Alphabet Deep Dive Analysis

Policy timing, crypto failure and Alphabet.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Wrong · 5Mixed · 7Right · 11Too early · 2Not testable · 15Conditional · 2Evidence needed · 1
This report contains several unusually testable calls. The Fed-pause deadline and six-to-twelve-month Treasury thesis failed; the named deferred oil contract also settled below its quoted entry reference. Alphabet and Bitcoin appreciated over the first three years of their three-to-five-year theses, with substantial interim volatility. Core Scientific's bankruptcy is a material company-selection miss. The Terra discussion largely repeats an already successful prior warning and should not inflate forecast counts. Conditional risks and valuation opinions are retained without forcing binary grades.
Coverage. All article text blocks were read. Ledger covers material macro, investment, and causal propositions; incidental descriptive statistics, boilerplate, and unreasoned position lists are excluded. Image labels were read, not image pixels.
