132-C02 · explanation · source blocks 15, 18

Weaker dollar relieves external debt.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsUnhedged dollar liabilities
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Local debt service equals e*D. A lower e reduces this cost by D times the exchange-rate change.

Assumptions: Dollar debt payment D is fixed; Exchange rate e is local currency per dollar; No hedge, unchanged local revenue

For unchanged local revenues, a fall in the local-currency cost of dollar debt reduces servicing burden mechanically. Actual investment performance also depends on hedges and earnings.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: Supported for the defined proposition.

Timing: No fixed deadline stated.

Mechanism: Narrow deductive identity only; no empirical forecasting credit.

Reviewer confidence: high. Thesis family: dollar-em-transmission.

Independent evidence

This explanation is assessed through its stated derivation, not an observed forecast result.
