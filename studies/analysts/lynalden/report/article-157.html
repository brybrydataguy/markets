157-C30 · explanation · source blocks 158

Higher implied volatility raises premiums.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Black-Scholes vega equals S exp(-q T) phi(d 1) sqrt(T), positive for S, T>0. Increased implied volatility raises both put and call theoretical prices.

Assumptions: Standard no-arbitrage vanilla option model; Other pricing inputs held fixed

Under fixed underlying price, strike, maturity, rates and dividends, a standard vanilla option has positive vega. Realized volatility and already-sold option profit are different concepts.
