159-C05 · explanation · source blocks 29

TGA drawdown supports reserves.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Reserves = assets - TGA - other liabilities; a TGA decline increases reserves by the same amount.

Assumptions: Fed assets and other nonreserve liabilities fixed; Treasury payment credited through commercial-bank reserve accounts

Holding other Fed assets and liabilities fixed, reducing Treasury deposits increases another liability, including reserves when Treasury pays private bank accounts. Actual asset-price sensitivity is a separate empirical question.
