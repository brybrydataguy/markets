025-C23 · explanation · source blocks 141

Small positions contain tail losses

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

At weight w, value can decline from w times initial portfolio capital to zero. Direct loss is at most w times initial capital. Correlated losses elsewhere remain separate.

Assumptions: Unlevered position paid in full; No additional capital commitment, recourse liability or short exposure; Asset value cannot fall below zero

For an unlevered standalone sleeve, its loss cannot exceed its invested capital. This is a bounded portfolio identity, not evidence that a basket of cheap countries must have positive expected returns.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: Resolved as stated.

Timing: No fixed endpoint supplied.

Mechanism: Portfolio loss contribution is position weight times asset loss, absent leverage or correlated additional exposures.

Reviewer confidence: high. Thesis family: position-size-tail-risk.

Independent evidence

This explanation is assessed through its stated derivation, not an observed forecast result.
