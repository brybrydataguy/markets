Portfolio Pruning

Portfolio simplification and a defined Tesla short.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Mixed · 8Evidence needed · 3Not testable · 2Wrong · 1
The Tesla setup failed its stated stop condition after an initial favorable move. Five-year sensitivities sharply separate successful Facebook exposure from losing Alibaba and JD exposure. Portfolio simplification and income improvement require holdings-level reconstruction, so they are not credited as automatic performance gains.
