Liquidity Pivot and Banking Update

Liquidity turning point and bank resilience.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Right · 5Not testable · 10Mixed · 11Evidence needed · 4Wrong · 1
The updated liquidity pivot was timely: QT ended and reserve-management buying began in December. Banking resilience was supported by subsequent data, but the treatment of undrawn lines as not yet at risk is incorrect. Bitcoin failed to follow the simple bullish liquidity narrative on a disclosed annual sensitivity.
