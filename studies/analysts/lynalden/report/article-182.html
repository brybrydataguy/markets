182-C03 · explanation · source blocks 14

Weaker dollars help foreign borrowers.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Local liability value is USD liability times local units/USD; dollar depreciation reduces that product.

Assumptions: Fixed USD liabilities; Local-currency revenues unchanged; Unhedged exposure

The local-currency cost of fixed dollar debt falls when the dollar depreciates, assuming unchanged local earnings and no offsetting hedge. This bounded balance-sheet channel does not prove all emerging markets benefit equally.

182-C17 · explanation · source blocks 105, 141, 143

AI capex reduces distributable cash.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Increasing the subtracted capex term reduces current free cash flow one-for-one; future cash flows are not constrained by this identity.

Assumptions: Operating cash flow fixed; Free cash flow defined as operating cash flow less capex

For a given operating cash flow, higher capital spending reduces current free cash flow arithmetically. The identity does not establish poor lifetime returns: future revenue, depreciation, utilization and financing must be evaluated.
