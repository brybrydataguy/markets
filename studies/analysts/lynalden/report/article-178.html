178-C18 · explanation · source blocks 141, 142, 143

Dollar weakness eases emerging-market debt burdens.

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Local-currency obligation equals dollars owed times local currency per dollar; a lower conversion rate reduces the burden.

Assumptions: Fixed dollar obligation; Local-currency income; No offsetting dollar assets or hedge

For an unhedged borrower earning local currency and owing fixed dollars, dollar depreciation lowers the local-currency debt burden. Export revenues, hedges and local borrowing can offset or reverse exposure. A weaker exchange rate is not itself proof of abundant funding everywhere.

Analytical tools and framework

  • SOFR-IORB and repo usage: Measure average normalization and calendar spikes separately (image not visually inspected)
  • Fed assets/GDP projection: Scenario assumptions are not policy commitments (image not visually inspected)
  • Terra endogenous support ratios: Market capitalization is not liquid collateral (image not visually inspected)
  • Bank Treasury/agency holdings: Keep Treasury debt distinct from agency MBS (image not visually inspected)
  • Commodity relative-price ratios: Useful comparison, insufficient valuation rule (image not visually inspected)
