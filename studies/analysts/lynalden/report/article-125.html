125-C30 · explanation · source blocks 89

Flat dividend compounding

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Shares after n years = initial shares * (1.1)^n; dividend income scales with share count.

Assumptions: Fixed price P and annual dividend 0.1P; Immediate costless reinvestment; No taxes or default

At an unchanged price with all fixed earnings paid and dividends reinvested without costs, a 10% yield comillion pounds share count at 10%. That equivalence fails if price, payout or tax assumptions change.

125-C37 · explanation · source blocks 100

Inventories finite

Right
HorizonNot specified
ConditionsNot specified
Author modalityasserted
Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

Remaining stock after t periods is S-d*t; at t=S/d the initial stock is exhausted.

Assumptions: Initial usable stock S; Constant positive net draw d; No replenishment

A finite stock cannot sustain a constant net draw forever without replenishment. This arithmetic places no near-term lower bound on oil prices, because production and demand can adjust.
