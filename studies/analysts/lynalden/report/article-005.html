Lukoil Deep-Dive Analysis

Russian oil value, shale fragility and macro expectations.

Mixed · 10Evidence needed · 5Not testable · 4Right · 3
The inflation and shale-financing directions were successful, while the dollar and oil paths were much less smooth than the base case. The decisive investment weakness was underweighting access risk, not failure to notice it. Domestic Lukoil profitability cannot substitute for the actual ADR holder's recovery.
