Tencent Deep Dive Analysis

Liquidity, collateral lending, and Tencent timing.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Evidence needed · 8Right · 11Not testable · 19Mixed · 12Wrong · 12Conditional · 2Too early · 3
Several measurable forecasts failed: Treasury maturity, RRP peak, Q1 crypto direction, MARA put and Tencent bottom. Inflation persistence and taper timing worked, and utility mining colocation became commercial. The lending discussion correctly names liquidation cascades but overrelies on management sophistication and off-exchange custody as protection. The liquidity framework is useful only when issuance composition and actual facility counterparties are checked.
Coverage. Read all blocks 0-217 and 22 image labels. Includes monetary mechanics, maturity/refinancing forecasts, crypto timing/lending/mining risks, China legal structure, Tencent trade, MARA put and energy horizon. Promotional announcements distinguished from deployed scale.

Annual interest change = D * dr for refinanced debt. Coupons on debt not refinanced remain unchanged.
