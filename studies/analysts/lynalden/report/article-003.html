Cheesecake Factory Deep-Dive Analysis

Restaurant growth, acquisition economics and cash returns.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Not testable · 7Mixed · 5Evidence needed · 4Wrong · 3Right · 3
This article contains both a specific caution about severe equity downside and overconfident language about dividend durability and low risk. The first is not erased by the second. Acquisition expansion later materialized, but financing stress and payout suspension are decisive adverse evidence. The option mechanics include a factual mistake independent of investment performance.
Coverage. All numbered text blocks and image labels read. Material theses, forecasts and explanations are included; routine holdings lists, incidental statistics, company descriptions and boilerplate are excluded. Chart pixels were not inspected.

003-C21 · explanation · source blocks 172, 173

In-money puts necessarily exercise before expiration

Wrong
Horizonbefore March 20, 2020 expiration
ConditionsShare price below $40
Author modalityasserted

Touching or trading below strike before expiration does not force exercise. The holder controls early exercise, and assignment depends on exercise and allocation. The cash-secured strategy can still be coherent without this incorrect certainty.

Direction, timing, mechanism and confidence

Direction: See reason; no return inferred.

Timing: Original horizon retained: before March 20, 2020 expiration

Mechanism: Outcome evidence does not independently identify causation.

Reviewer confidence: high. Thesis family: option-assignment-mechanics.

Independent evidence
  • Options assignment FAQ
    American-style exercise is the holder's decision before expiration. At expiration, exercise-by-exception thresholds and contrary instructions apply; going in the money does not itself compel early assignment.
