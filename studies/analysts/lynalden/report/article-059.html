Roku (ROKU) and Etsy (ETSY) Deep Dive Analysis

Crypto developments and growth-stock purchases.

Open original members article ↗ · Retrieved November 7, 2026
Mixed · 15Not testable · 21Wrong · 8Right · 14Evidence needed · 6
This article contains meaningful misses that must remain visible beside successful crypto calls. June CPI exceeded the range; Etsy failed its 20% growth premise; both selected growth stocks lost roughly 60% over five years despite Roku business expansion. BTC beat ETH over the five-year comparison. ETH worked through year-end 2021 but failed over the separate year-ahead horizon. Technical progress in Lightning and Taproot should not be counted as proof of mass payment adoption. The Roku market-cap target is closed by acquisition, with the terminal-event qualification disclosed.
Coverage. Read every macro, crypto, portfolio and stock-analysis block and image label. Retained distinct horizons and technical mechanisms; excluded incidental biographies and routine position sizes. Source descriptions compressed to preserve comprehensive material coverage.

ConditionsNot specified

Deductive reasoning, not an empirical outcome test

PV=sum(CF_t/(1+r)^t); d PV/dr=-sum(t*CF_t/(1+r)^(t+1))<0.

Assumptions: Positive fixed future cash flows; discount rate increases uniformly; no offsetting growth revision.

The narrow discount-rate relationship holds if expected cash flows remain unchanged. Actual share movements also reflect earnings revisions and risk premia, so no causal market attribution is implied.
