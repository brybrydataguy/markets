The Silent Bull Market in Dividends

Income stocks, reserve pressure and a less cautious gold stance.

Analytical tools and framework

  • Dividend versus price returns: Include reinvestment, ADR fees and tax assumptions (image not visually inspected)
  • FAST Graphs and payout histories: Distribution yield is not risk-adjusted expected return (image not visually inspected)
  • Bank cash and domestic liquidity: Distribution of reserves matters alongside totals (image not visually inspected)
  • Gold share of reserves: Separate physical purchases from price appreciation (image not visually inspected)

A high yield is useful only alongside sustainable cash flow and tolerable downside.
