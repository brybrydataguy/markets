Concrete successes include the April TGA sequence, 2023 gold record, uranium breakout, 24-month Bitcoin gain and Schwab profit pressure. The continuing recession lean and asserted inability to sustain positive real rates fail. Schwab equity caution did not predict its later recovery, and the optional short stop would have been reached. Insurance and bank value opinions are kept separate from horizon-defined forecasts.
Coverage. All article text blocks were read. Ledger covers material macro, investment, and causal propositions; incidental descriptive statistics, boilerplate, and unreasoned position lists are excluded. Image labels were read, not image pixels.
